# Olive & Theo portfolio RSVP

Mode: Experience, with an Operate RSVP. User explicitly delegates original design and fictional content. Code-led per repository configuration. Scope: app/olive-and-theo only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A garden lunch invitation unfolds into a generous, photograph-led celebration, with a reply as personal and approachable as a handwritten note.

OWN-WORLD: Olive green, parchment, warm clay, Instrument Serif and readable Inter; circular date seal, hairline rules, panoramic tablescape, plain tactile controls. Reuse existing fonts and form semantics, not another client's identity.

STORY: Guests open the invitation, discover the fictional March 20, 2027 wedding at The Willow House, view the date, venue and countdown, then complete a staged in-memory RSVP.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Opaque olive opening with centered serif message and Open button. Foreground fades upward over its opaque backing; after foreground completion, the backing dissolves into the destination. Destination: spacious parchment navigation, large centered Olive & Theo, a panoramic photograph and a clay date seal. Primary RSVP link remains visible in navigation and hero.

FORM: Original garden-party invitation, direct authoring under the user's free creative brief; no concept selection required by user. Signature interaction: a lifting invitation cover followed by a staged RSVP review and editable confirmation. Reduced motion hands off immediately.

Process exception: The user delegated original creative direction and fictional sample content. This run directly authored the invitation instead of conducting the skill's concept tournament. No concept-roll seed, QUALITY BAR card, or approved comp exists, and none is claimed retroactively. Finish review assesses the built composition, originality, usability, and this contract; it cannot score a missing card's ceiling.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Follow-up scope: User removed itinerary, quote interlude, FAQs/attire, and all visible disclaimer messages. Add live countdown and RSVP metadata (100 capacity, one person maximum, March 1 deadline, seats counter). Preserve original design and frontend-only operation.

RSVP follow-up: Full name, optional contact number, accept/decline attendance, and one submit button only. Remove email, meal/dietary/note fields and the intermediate review step. Keep current styling, capacity, countdown, and in-memory behavior.
