# Jaydee & Bea · Whimsical Garden

Route: `/jaydee-and-bea`. This is a mobile-first invitation built using the existing Next.js App Router, Tailwind, and Lucide. Details and body copy use the requested Noto Serif, self-hosted as normal and italic Latin WOFF2 files under `assets/fonts`, with weights 400–600 and the accompanying license. Headings retain the root layout’s Instrument Serif and Imperial Script. These heading fonts are provisional draft choices. Client implementation and media configuration live in this folder; raster artwork and photos use verified public R2 uploads. No dependencies were added.

## Updating content

`data/wedding-data.ts` is the source of truth for event content, venues, timeline, story, entourage, attire, gallery, RSVP, music, hashtag, palette, and pending values. Types live in `types/wedding.ts`. `page.tsx` composes focused sections. Simple layout uses Tailwind; decorative, typographic, and animation styles are scoped by `styles/wedding.module.css`.

The entourage follows the supplied reference with a centered pavilion ornament, script headings, a leafy divider, flowering vines along the edges, and compact serif names on a paper-textured background. Artwork is configured in `data/design-media.ts`: `gardern-arch.webp`, `vines-1.webp`, `flower-vines.webp`, and `flower-vines-2.webp`. Parents and roles use paired columns, while `memberColumns` explicitly preserves the principal sponsors’ two-column order. Groups are displayed openly with headings and lists, without dropdowns. Confirmed names are always visible; pending bridesmaid and groomsman groups remain hidden until supplied.

The hero follows the supplied design reference with stacked script names and R2 artwork configured in `data/design-media.ts`: `gradient-bg.webp`, `pink-flowers.webp`, `pink-flowers-3.webp`, `whimsical-entrance-place.webp`, and `couples.webp`. `components/HeroGardenArtwork.tsx` composes the supplied garden and couple illustrations. Next Image and responsive sizes serve the verified R2 artwork with its original dimensions. Other sections retain the unchanged `assets/garden-spray.svg`. The garden and couple are decorative illustrations, not venue or couple photographs. Confirmed wedding details remain the source of truth for the hero text.

The story follows the supplied photo layout: the complete R2 floral portrait artwork (`our-story-placeholder-pic.webp`, configured in `data/design-media.ts`), a two-line script heading, Noto Serif prose, a floral divider, a wide photo, and garden urns on a paper-textured background. On small screens, text wraps beneath the portrait to remain readable. All four third-person paragraphs render in their original chronological order: first meeting, relationship, proposal, then wedding. `data/story-photos.ts` holds the requested portrait artwork and the R2 landscape placeholder (`Group1-1.webp`, configured in `data/prenup-media.ts`), alternative text, and landscape crop position. These are placeholders, not confirmed photos of Jaydee and Bea; update the media configuration and alt text when final images are approved. The existing photo gallery is configured separately.

The timeline follows the supplied arch layout with a pastel backdrop, pink floral borders, a script heading, line-art milestone icons, and a continuous heart-marked stem. `components/TimelineGardenArch.tsx` renders a decorative trellis with leafy vines that adapts to the event list’s height. Each centered time and title summarizes the original schedule without a separate description. Icon choices are typed metadata in the centralized timeline data; the confirmed event times are unchanged. Decorations are hidden from assistive technology, and the schedule remains an ordered list with event headings.

The wedding is January 16, 2027, with ceremony at 3:00 PM Philippine time (`Asia/Manila`, UTC+8). The countdown targets the ceremony, clamps to zero after it, and stops ticking. The server initially renders a dash to avoid hydration mismatches.

The supplied pasted request is authoritative. The questionnaire is source evidence; its developer-directed notes do not override the request. Story prose is an English adaptation of its confirmed facts. The contact email in the questionnaire is not published.
